COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about mashq tests, scoring, and AI assistance.

How does the test scoring and negative marking work? +
Tests mirror official JKSSB marking rules: each correct answer earns +1.0 mark, and each incorrect answer deducts -0.25 marks. Unanswered questions do not affect your score.
Can I pause a test and finish it later? +
Yes! Click the Pause button at any time during an active test. Your remaining time, answered questions, and review flags are securely saved to your cloud account so you can resume on any device.
What is the TestWise AI Study Tutor? +
TestWise AI is an integrated study companion powered by Google Gemini. After submitting a test, click "Ask AI Tutor" on any question to ask follow-up questions, request mnemonics, or receive step-by-step concept explanations.
How do the 12 Brand Accent Palettes work? +
Open Settings (⚙️) from the top bar or sidebar to select from 12 curated scholar palettes (such as Classic Ink & Verdigris, Royal Qalam & Gold, or Sumi Ink & Saffron) across Light, Dark, and Lights-Out themes.
Is payment required to take tests right now? +
The $99 course plan displayed on this page is a demo structural placeholder. In this current preview version, you can simply create a free account or sign in with Google to take tests.

OFFICIAL SYLLABUS BREAKDOWN

Detailed Topic Weightage & Syllabus Modules

The FAA examination comprises 120 multiple choice questions distributed across six core subject areas.

📑

Accountancy & Bookkeeping

30 Marks • 25% of Paper
  • Introduction to Financial Accounting & Terms
  • Accounting Principles, Concepts & Conventions
  • Voucher Approach in Accounting
  • Journal, Ledger, Cash Book & Subsidiary Books
  • Bank Reconciliation Statement (BRS)
  • Trial Balance & Rectification of Errors
  • Financial Statements: Trading, P&L, Balance Sheet
  • Partnership Accounts (Admission, Retirement, Dissolution)
  • Auditing: Meaning, Objective & Types
  • PFMS, GeM Portal & Public Financial Rules
🏔️

General Knowledge (Special Focus on UT of J&K)

30 Marks • 25% of Paper
  • Current Events of National and International Importance
  • Political & Physical Divisions of India & World
  • Climate, Crops & Agriculture in India
  • History, Geography & Economy of UT of Jammu & Kashmir
  • J&K Reorganisation Act, 2019 (Key Provisions & Schedules)
  • Flora, Fauna, Lakes & Rivers of J&K
  • Historical Places, Heritage & Tourist Destinations of J&K
  • Centrally Sponsored Schemes & Regional Initiatives
💻

Knowledge of Computer Applications

20 Marks • 16.7% of Paper
  • Basic Applications of Computer & its Components
  • Fundamentals of Hardware & Software
  • Input & Output Devices, Storage Media
  • Operating Systems (Windows, Linux basics)
  • MS Office Suite: MS Word, MS Excel, MS PowerPoint
  • Internet, Web Browsing, Email, Search Engines
  • Computer Viruses, Antivirus & Cyber Security Basics
🔬

General Science

10 Marks • 8.3% of Paper
  • Newton's Laws of Motion, Work, Power & Energy
  • Electric Current, Resistance, Voltage & Circuits
  • Chemical Reactions, Acids, Bases & Salts
  • Metals and Non-Metals in Everyday Life
  • Structure of Atom, Nutrition, Respiration & Excretion
  • Communicable & Non-Communicable Diseases, Vaccines
  • Environmental Pollution, Ozone Depletion & Greenhouse Effect
📐

Mathematics & Statistics

10 Marks • 8.3% of Paper
  • Theory of Probability (Basic Events & Compound Probability)
  • Tabulation, Compilation & Graphical Representation of Data
  • Measures of Central Tendency (Mean, Median, Mode)
  • Measures of Dispersion (Range, Standard Deviation)
  • Set Theory, Relations & Functions
  • Matrices & Determinants (Basic Computations)
  • Coordinate Geometry & Simple Equations
📈

General Economics

10 Marks • 8.3% of Paper
  • Introduction to Economics, Demand & Supply Analysis
  • Elasticity of Demand & Indifference Curves
  • National Income: GDP, GNP, NNP & Per Capita Income
  • Inflation: Types, Causes & Control Mechanisms
  • Fiscal Policy, Budget Formulation & Deficits
  • Monetary Policy, RBI Functions & Banking Structure
  • Economic Reforms in India since 1991

Under which Schedule of the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 are the forms of oaths or affirmations for members of the Legislative Assembly and Ministers specified?
A Second Schedule
B Third Schedule
C Fourth Schedule (Correct Answer ✓) ✓
D Fifth Schedule
Detailed Solution:
The Fourth Schedule of the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 prescribes the forms of oaths or affirmations to be taken by Ministers, members of the Legislative Assembly, and the Chief Minister of the Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir.
